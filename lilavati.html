Logical Framework

Tarka Sangraha: Core Definitions

Definition of Padarthatvam
ऽभिधेयत्वं पदार्थसामान्यलक्षणम्
The general characteristic of being a knowable/nameable entity (Padartha) is its knowability.
Definition of Avyapti (Too Narrow Definition)
लक्ष्यैकदेशावृत्तित्वमव्याप्तिः
Avyapti occurs when a definition fails to apply to a part of the intended target class.
Definition of Ativyapti (Overly Broad Definition)
अलक्ष्यवृत्तित्वमतिव्याप्तिः लक्षणस्य वर्तनमतिव्याप्तिः
Ativyapti occurs when a definition improperly extends to items outside the intended target class.
Definition of Asambhava (Impossible Definition)
लक्ष्यमात्राऽवर्तनमसम्भवः
Asambhava occurs when the defined characteristic does not exist anywhere within the target class.
Definition of Lakshanam (Valid Definition)
एतद्दूषणत्रयरहितो धर्मो लक्षणम्
A valid definition is a characteristic completely free from the three logical flaws (Avyapti, Ativyapti, and Asambhava).
Definition of Asaadhaaranatvam
लक्ष्यतावच्छेदकसमनियतत्वमसाधारणत्वम्
The quality of being an extraordinary or exclusive qualifier coterminous with the target boundary.
Definition of Lakshanam (Refined)
व्यावृत्तावभिधेयत्वादौ चातिव्याप्तिवारणाय तद्भिन्नत्वं धर्मविशेषणं
Refined to exclude ativyapti from vyavrutti by introducing distinct qualifying conditions.
Definition of Dravyam (Substance)
द्रव्यत्वजातिमत्त्वं गुणवत्त्वं वा द्रव्यसामान्यलक्षणम्
The general definition of a substance is possessing the universal attribute of 'Dravyatva' or possessing qualities (guna).
Definition of Dravyam (Refined)
गुणसमानाधिकरणसत्ताभिन्नजातिमत्त्वम्
Refined definition to resolve the logical flaw where qualities do not exist simultaneously with the substance during its initial creation and final destruction.
