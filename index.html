The Language

Why Samskritam?

Samskritam is more than just an ancient language - it is the key to India's vast heritage of knowledge. By learning it, we can directly read original texts on science, mathematics, logic, and philosophy without losing meaning in translation.

For the IISc community, this language holds special value. Its highly logical, math-like grammar improves analytical thinking, while its precise pronunciation offers a sense of focus and calm in our busy academic lives.

Our Mission

Objectives of the Sangha

  • To cultivate a welcoming environment for students, faculty, and friends to learn and speak Samskritam on campus.
  • To bridge the gap between ancient Indian knowledge systems and modern scientific research.
  • To organize study circles, workshops, and expert talks that make Samskritam accessible to everyone, from beginners to advanced learners.
